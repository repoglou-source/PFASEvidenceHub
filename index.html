FAQ

Questions about PFAS-free claims

Does a “PFAS-free” label automatically prove a product is PFAS-free? +

Not necessarily. A claim and the evidence supporting that claim are different things. PFAS Evidence Hub is designed to make the available supporting information and remaining questions easier to identify.

What kind of evidence can be recorded? +

Depending on the product, this may include manufacturer statements, supplier responses, product documentation, test information or other relevant evidence.

Is PFAS Evidence Hub a certification scheme? +

The MVP is intended as an evidence and information layer. It should not be interpreted as automatically certifying or independently testing every product listed.

Why focus specifically on PFAS? +

Because consumers often need a very specific answer: what evidence supports a product's PFAS-free claim, rather than a general sustainability or eco-friendly statement.

Can organisations contribute information? +

Yes. The MVP is particularly interested in conversations with organisations, researchers, consumer groups and product stakeholders.
